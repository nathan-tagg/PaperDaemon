Third-party code and algorithms in fastdecomp
=============================================

fastdecomp reproduces, bit for bit, what a Python reference computes with these libraries. Where their code is
VENDORED (copied) it keeps its own notice and license file; where an algorithm is PORTED (re-implemented to give the
same results) the file says so at its top. The licenses follow.

Vendored
--------
* Pillow 12.3.0 (MIT-CMU / HPND) -- vendor/pillow/Quant.c, QuantHash.c, QuantHash.h, QuantHeap.c, QuantHeap.h,
  QuantTypes.h: the median-cut quantizer (src/libImaging). Quant.c is trimmed as its header says (ImagingQuantize, the
  octree/libimagequant includes and the unused maximum-coverage method removed; quantize() renamed). vendor/pillow/LICENSE
  is Pillow's license file.
* stb_image v2.30 (public domain or MIT, at the user's choice) -- vendor/stb/stb_zlib.c: stb_image.h's zlib decoder
  section (commit f58f558c120e9b32c217290b80bad1a0729fbb2c, lines 4082-4594) copied unchanged under a short prelude,
  for decode.c. vendor/stb/LICENSE holds its license text.
* libjpeg-turbo 3.1.4.1 (IJG License, Modified BSD License, zlib License: see vendor/libjpeg-turbo/LICENSE.md and
  README.ijg) -- vendor/libjpeg-turbo/src: its decompression sources and headers, unchanged, and its 8/12/16-bit
  wrapper files; jconfig.h, jconfigint.h and jversion.h written from its templates (see jconfig.h). This software is
  based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group. Used by jpeg.c.
* x86-simd-sort (BSD-3-Clause, Copyright (c) 2022, Intel) -- vendor/x86-simd-sort/src: the headers numpy 2.5.3 builds
  against (its source distribution's numpy/_core/src/npysort/x86-simd-sort), unchanged; vendor/x86-simd-sort/LICENSE.md
  is its license file. npsort_avx2.cpp compiles its 64-bit argsort for numpy's X86_V3 target.
* LLVM libc++ (Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception) -- linked into the library (from the zig toolchain) for npsort_avx2.cpp,
  the one C++ file; the LLVM exception covers its use in a compiled binary.

Ported
------
* Pillow 12.3.0 (MIT-CMU / HPND): blur.c (BoxBlur.c: ImagingBoxBlur, ImagingGaussianBlur and its radius), the RGB to L
  conversion (Convert.c rgb2l) in zones.c, tonal.c, stats.c, quant.c, xdog.c, ImageOps.autocontrast in xdog.c,
  mediancut.c (ImagingQuantize's RGB part), decode.c (PngImagePlugin's chunk handling and modes, Unpack.c's PNG
  unpackers, Convert.c's conversions to RGB, the palette default of Palette.c).
* SciPy 1.18.1 (BSD-3-Clause): quant.c and merge.c (ndimage NI_UniformFilter1D), flow.c (NI_Correlate1D,
  gaussian_filter), label.c / regions.c / specks.c (ndimage.label's numbering), specks.c (ndimage.center_of_mass's sums),
  tonal.c (distance_transform_edt's exact distances), merge.c (sparse.csgraph.connected_components' numbering).
* NumPy 2.5 (BSD-3-Clause): npsort.c (the scalar aquicksort_ and aheapsort_ of quicksort.hpp / npysort_heapsort.h, and
  the argsort dispatch rule), palette.c and inkpass.c (the scalar ** 2, the linear quantile), npsum.c (the pairwise
  float64 sum), zones.c (linear percentile, median), stats.c (median,
  remainder, rad2deg), lab.c (elementwise operation order), merge.c (lexsort and stable argsort orders, unique's counts,
  floor_divide), the NEP 50 scalar promotion rules in pass.c and store.c.
* OpenCV (Apache-2.0): plan.c (distransform.cpp trueDistTrans, approx.cpp approxPolyDP for float points,
  shapedescr.cpp contourArea).
* scikit-image (BSD-3-Clause): plan.c (measure.find_contours: marching squares and contour assembly).
* CPython 3.12 (PSF-2.0): outline.c (math.hypot's vector_norm, the builtin sum's compensated summation), zones.c and
  stats.c (math.atan2's m_atan2 rule, float %), pass.c (float floor division), hatch.c (math.radians, hypot),
  model.c (round(x, 1): the correctly rounded decimal).
