# Chess pieces: the "cburnett" set

The twelve chess piece drawings in this folder are the "cburnett" set by Colin M.L. Burnett, as published by
Lichess: <https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett>.

They are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license (CC BY-SA 3.0):
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>. This license applies to these drawings and to the piece
pictures PaperDaemon renders from them, not to the rest of PaperDaemon, which is under the Apache License 2.0.

The files here are unchanged. PaperDaemon recolors the pieces when it draws them
(`paperdaemon/pipeline/games/chess/pieces.py`) to match its chess board: the white pieces' fill is #F7F7F2,
the black pieces' fill #1C1C1C, their light details #F4F4EC, and every outline #111111. The recolored
pictures are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 3.0.
