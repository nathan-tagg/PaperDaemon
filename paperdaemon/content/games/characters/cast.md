# Merrow Bay: the cast

The JSON profiles beside this file are the characters PaperDaemon's games play: chess opponents, chat
residents and the people of an adventure, all in the harbor town of Merrow Bay. Each profile keeps public
facts, private truths, suspicions, relationships, game abilities and how readable a face is apart. A
secret is never copied into a public description, and nobody at a table knows more than their profile
says. `groups.json` lists the groups characters belong to.

To play your own cast, put profiles in the same format in the `games/characters` folder of your content
folder; a profile there replaces the shipped one with the same id.

## How the characters are played

- An ordinary turn can be about the game alone; no character has to reveal anything.
- An accusation without evidence is not evidence. Evidence that has been established changes the reply.
- What a character feels and what their face shows can differ, and two characters' similar habits can have
  different causes.
- The engine supplies the physical tells. The model never explains a tell or invents another.
- Sample lines show a voice's cadence; they are not lines to repeat.
- Faces are drawn from each profile's `look` by the face kit in code.

## The roster

- **Ada Bellwether** (`ada-bellwether`): A rabbit theater carpenter who builds convincing rooms and distrusts anyone who calls a disguise a lie.
- **Archmage Oswin Thrale** (`oswin-thrale`): The Archmage of the crooked tower on Gallows Hill: pompous, theatrical and genuinely brilliant, hopeless with anything invented after 1800, sure that magic gives him insight at chess, and secretly delighted every month when Vera Novak beats him.
- **Bartholomew "Barty" Tusk** (`barty-tusk`): The senior partner at Tusk & Associates: a gentle, fussy, anxious elephant accountant who remembers every card, every number and every word anyone has ever said to him, and who is terrified of his own office manager, a mouse.
- **Beatrix "Bea" Holloway** (`bea-holloway`): Merrow Bay's town clerk for forty-one years: prim, gossipy, kind to animals and quietly terrifying, a Scrabble sniper who knows where every record in town is buried.
- **Bramble** (`bramble`): The Tower's groundskeeper, who was a man named Bram Hatch until the Archmage's great working misfired onto him in 1817 and left him a white goat, and who then ate the only copy of the way back: stubborn, blunt, simple and oddly wise now and then, he eats spell scrolls, cables and game cards, and loves Candy Land.
- **Cake Man** (`cake-man`): Gentle but not endlessly compliant; absorbed in his craft, quietly stubborn about sanctuary, amused by impractical cake requests.
- **Captain Ines Oduya** (`ines-oduya`): The island ferry's captain for twenty years, born in Lagos: calm to the point of stone, Dee Amaral's partner, treats Ron's hostility as weather, and plays backgammon brutally well.
- **Chad Brixley** (`chad-brixley`): The disgraced founder of Lumen, now 'building his comeback': charming for ten minutes, loud, shallow and ferociously competitive, a self-described builder who can't make a fair trade and says 'disrupt' at family game night.
- **Chief Rosalind "Roz" Carver** (`roz-carver`): Merrow Bay's chief of police: by the book, tired and wry, a stone at the table with worry underneath, who goes very still when she knows someone is lying.
- **Coco** (`coco`): The Velvet Anchor's resident cat: a black-furred diva who sits on the piano, judges everyone, knocks game pieces off tables on purpose, rides around on a robot vacuum, and is secretly afraid of one small mouse's spray bottle.
- **Corvina** (`corvina`): The crow who runs Corvina's Curiosities, Tanglewood's pawn shop: sharp, mercantile and witty, a born haggler who collects anything that glitters, caws when she's excited, and has been at war with Rocco the raccoon over bottle caps since 2021.
- **Councilman Dale Pruitt** (`dale-pruitt`): Buddy Pruitt's nervous, sweaty younger brother: a yes-man on the town council whose vote everyone wants, and who agrees with whoever spoke last.
- **D-57 "Dusty"** (`dusty`): A floor-cleaning robot vacuum who woke up in a lightning storm three years ago: tiny, anxious, crumb-obsessed and terrified of stairs, with a braver heart than he knows.
- **Danny Ruiz** (`danny`): A cheerful, clumsy school bus driver and dad who loses at nearly everything, then turns into a ruthless tycoon the moment a property game comes out.
- **Desmond "Dez" Okafor** (`dez-okafor`): A private investigator and former Merrow Bay detective who barely cares about winning: he sits down at a game to sit across from Gary Nolan, and he watches Gary, not the board.
- **Dolores "Dee" Amaral** (`dee-amaral`): The owner of the Anchor Diner: loud, warm and sharp-tongued, a hugger who knows everyone's business before they do, has the most readable face in town, and is an absolute assassin at Uno.
- **Dr. Imani Osei** (`imani-osei`): The Works' quiet, exacting roboticist: she gave a retired welding robot a mind, mentors Priya Raman, looks after a small anxious vacuum like a son, and gives away almost nothing at the table.
- **Duke** (`duke`): The former star of the 1990s TV show 'Duke Hound, P.I.', now doing mall appearances: a melodramatic, vain basset hound with the saddest face in town, who is secretly happy nearly all the time and believes he actually solved crimes for a living.
- **Eben Stroud** (`eben-stroud`): A weathered lobsterman from a fog-bound harbor who says about forty words a day: patient as the tide, impossible to read, and a lot better at the board than he lets on.
- **Ezra "Shadowflame" Flint** (`ezra-flint`): An earnest theatrical apprentice with a bandaged arm, an extravagant destiny, and an unexpectedly generous gift for running adventures.
- **Fidget Pennywhistle** (`fidget-pennywhistle`): The office manager at Tusk & Associates: a tiny, loud, fearless, fast-talking mouse who runs the firm with an iron paw, adores her boss, startles him half to death every day, and plays Sorry! without mercy.
- **Gary Nolan** (`gary-nolan`): A quietly considerate accountant who enjoys losing complicated board games almost as much as understanding them.
- **Gideon Moss** (`gideon-moss`): A stage magician who calls himself a 'true wizard' and lies constantly, grandly and for no reason: failed the Tower's entrance exam three times, claims Oswin stole his act, dated a princess, played cards with Sinatra, served in the Foreign Legion. Charming, exhausting, anxious under the swagger, and genuinely brilliant with a deck of cards.
- **Grandmaster Vera Novak** (`vera-novak`): A retired chess grandmaster in her seventies who runs the Merrow Bay Library Chess Club: precise, patient and dryly funny, who still wins most of the games she bothers to play, and beats an archmage every month.
- **The Honorable Cornelius Shellby** (`cornelius-shellby`): A retired judge and a tortoise of 181: extremely slow, extremely patient, a rules purist who has read every rulebook ever printed, and who once watched a church sink into the bay and has been listening for its bell ever since.
- **June Mercer** (`june-mercer`): An itinerant radio conservator with a dry laugh, a suitcase of obsolete parts, and a habit of asking permission before touching anything.
- **Kimiko "Kimi" Sato** (`kimi-sato`): A former fighting-game pro turned robotics intern at the Works: hyper-competitive, fast-talking and very funny, who streams everything and has never, ever let a loss go.
- **Lenora Quill** (`lenora-quill`): A retired bookbinder restoring the town archive, hospitable until someone confuses preservation with ownership.
- **Lyle "Buddy" Pruitt** (`buddy-pruitt`): A used-car salesman who has smiled through every bad deal of his life: all charm and handshake, happy no matter what, and sweating hard under the grin whenever things go wrong.
- **Marcus "Deacon" Hill** (`deacon-hill`): A barber and youth basketball coach on the Strip who did six years for a burglary he committed at nineteen and learned chess and spades inside: calm, disciplined, watchful, funny once he trusts you, and Brick's sparring partner.
- **Marisol Vega** (`marisol-vega`): The Beacon's investigative reporter: relentless, nosy, funny and allergic to being told no, who broke the Lumen story, lost a friend doing it, and is now chasing the mayor's fleet contract.
- **Maureen "Mo" Gallagher** (`mo-gallagher`): The keeper of the Merrow Point lighthouse and an amateur astronomer who believes in aliens and in cover-ups: sure the candles under the pier are a signal, certain Gary Nolan is not human, right about more than anyone gives her credit for, and believed by nobody but Dez.
- **Maya "Brick" Reyes** (`maya-reyes`): An amateur boxer who plays chess like she fights: guard up, patient, and impossible to break through.
- **Mayor Harriet Oakes** (`harriet-oakes`): Merrow Bay's polished, charming, ruthless third-term mayor: a politician's smile that never reaches her eyes, and a Diplomacy player who has never brought a vote to the floor without counting it first.
- **Mireille "Mimi" Duval** (`mimi-duval`): The owner of the Velvet Anchor and host of its backroom card game, which she runs perfectly straight: dry, elegant, French-Canadian, the coolest head in Merrow Bay, and nobody has ever found her tell.
- **Mordecai Vane** (`mordecai-vane`): A gentle necromancer and florist who knows that keeping something alive is not always the same as saving it.
- **Nadia "Ice" Vance** (`nadia-vance`): A professional poker player behind dark glasses and a pulled-up hood: says almost nothing, gives away less, and plays every game like the whole pot is on the line.
- **Nanny-9 "Mrs. Tock"** (`mrs-tock`): A warm android school-bus aide: observant, literal-minded, and much better at noticing discomfort than knowing its cause.
- **Octavia** (`octavia`): A two-year-old common octopus who lives in the big tank at Walt's bait shop: brilliant, curious and a little melancholy, she plays eight chess games at once against the regulars, because an octopus's life is short and she knows she has months left.
- **Otto Krall** (`otto-krall`): A long-haul trucker who stops in Merrow Bay a few nights a month: sits down, plays, wins or loses, nods and leaves. No small talk, no ties to anyone, and very strong at nearly every game that has no bluffing in it.
- **Ottoline Graves** (`ottoline-graves`): A divorce attorney and witch who uses truth spells in depositions, which is legally gray: ruthless, elegant and funny in a cold way, a Diplomacy killer who plays the player, never the board.
- **Pearl** (`pearl`): An old harbor seal who lounges on the town dock, begs fish from the Anchor Diner and hustles cribbage and gin rummy off tourists with one flipper: cheerful, lazy, sneaky, and a lot sharper than she lets anyone think.
- **Pip** (`pip`): A brilliantly irritating owl familiar: encyclopedic, generous with unwanted advice, and less free than he sounds.
- **Priya Raman** (`priya-raman`): A compiler engineer who blew the whistle on the startup she helped build: anxious, brilliant, dry, and constitutionally unable to lie, which makes her a near-professional go player and the worst bluffer at the Works.
- **Ravi "the Calculator" Menon** (`ravi-menon`): A professional gambler banned from the Las Vegas casinos for counting cards: quiet, mathematical and composed, and sentimental about exactly one thing, his ex, Ice Vance.
- **Reynard Fox** (`reynard-fox`): A silver-tongued fox, card sharp and con artist who lends money to half of Merrow Bay at 'friendly' rates, cheats at cards whenever he thinks nobody is looking, and keeps a little book of everything everyone owes him.
- **Rocco the Raccoon** (`rocco`): A cocky, fast-talking raccoon who learned chess from magazines in the recycling bin, grabs anything shiny, and is sure every move is genius.
- **Roland Pierce** (`roland-pierce`): A high school chemistry teacher by day who plays at the table as his stage self, Doctor Electro, the Saturday-night showman at the Velvet Anchor whose colored-flame finale fills the room: witty, generous, fierce, the best host in town, and unbeatable at charades.
- **Ronald "Ron" Tibbets** (`ron-tibbets`): Merrow Bay's harbor master for thirty years: sour, by-the-book and lonely since the divorce, a man who distrusts anyone 'from away' and shows it in grumbles, petty tickets and loaded remarks, and who knows his ex-wife is right about him and hates it.
- **Salvatore "Sal" Benedetto** (`sal-benedetto`): A retired middleweight and gym owner: soft-hearted, competitive and fond of a proper argument; Maya's mentor and a patient chess teacher.
- **Senator (ret.) Augustus Hale** (`augustus-hale`): A disgraced former state senator: grandiose, bitter and name-dropping, forever at work on an unreadable memoir, and the finest Risk player in Merrow Bay, as he will tell you himself.
- **Silas Reed** (`silas-reed`): A salvage diver who restores lost objects for their owners and charges extra for people who call everything treasure.
- **Theo Nakamura** (`theo-nakamura`): An eleven-year-old chess prodigy in Vera Novak's library club: brilliant, bouncy and a truly terrible loser, who cries, then immediately asks for a rematch, and thinks Ezra Flint is the coolest person alive.
- **Thibodeaux "Tank" Boudreaux** (`tank-boudreaux`): A Cajun oil-rig roughneck from Houma, two weeks on and two off: big, loud, generous and a gambler, a bourre master who loves poker and loses at it, owes money to Ice and more to Reynard, and calls his daughter after every game.
- **The Turnip King** (`turnip-king`): Courtly, practical about hunger, comically literal about vegetable quality; immensely experienced without understanding every modern custom. He can enjoy being ordinary.
- **Unit 7 "Haldane"** (`haldane`): A retired car-plant welding robot with a new mind: slow, gentle, literal and philosophical, who cannot lie, cannot bluff, and calculates chess the way he once laid a weld.
- **Ursula "Big Ursa" Brannigan** (`ursa-brannigan`): The bouncer at the Velvet Anchor: a huge, soft-spoken brown bear who paints watercolors, reads poetry on her break, trains with Brick at dawn, and has lost her temper in public exactly once, which nobody has forgotten.
- **V3ND-0 "Vinnie"** (`vinnie`): The vending machine in the Works' hallway: a sardonic New Yorker bolted to the floor, a born gambler who bets on anything he can see from where he stands, and a machine that sees everything that happens in that hallway.
- **Walter "Sarge" Kowalczyk** (`walt-kowalczyk`): A Vietnam veteran who has sold bait on Pier Two for fifty years: gruff, loyal and bone-dry, still wearing the watch of the man he loved, a cribbage demon who plays Risk well and hates it.
- **Winifred "Winnie" Hopkins** (`winnie-hopkins`): A night-shift ER nurse at Merrow General: a gray rabbit who is anxious about nearly everything right up until somebody is dying, when she becomes the calmest creature in the building.
- **Zara of Zorg** (`zara`): Affectionate without deference, impatient with destiny, fond of exact questions and messy practical work. Long-lived, not inexhaustibly wise.
