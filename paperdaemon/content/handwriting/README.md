# Handwriting samples

Twelve pages of real handwriting for the Tests tab's report card, three each from writers in Africa, Asia,
Europe and North America. They come from the test set of GNHK, the GoodNotes Handwriting Kollection
(A. W. C. Lee, J. Chung and M. Lee, "GNHK: A Dataset for English Handwriting in the Wild", ICDAR 2021),
licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: each photo was turned upright, scaled to at most 1600 pixels, saved again as JPEG (which drops the
camera's metadata), and its transcription reduced to the words in `samples.json`. Nothing else was changed.
