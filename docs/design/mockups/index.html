PaperDaemon

Interface mockups

Static pages with invented example data, drawn in the interface language described in docs/design/ui-language.md. Every button, switch, menu and dialog works, so you can see what each one does; nothing is saved and nothing talks to an engine. tools/mockup_book.py makes the same pages into one PDF for the tablet.

  1. 01First runSign in through the reMarkable desktop app, pull your library, choose your models, start.›
  2. 02OverviewWhat the daemon noticed and what the host did, job by job; sync and model state.›
  3. 03NotepadsEach notebook's memory of its jobs, what the next job is shown, and wiping it.›
  4. 04TabletThe desktop app's sign-in, connection, PaperDaemon's copy of the library, tags, the inbox notebook, rolling back writes.›
  5. 05ModelsLineups: the two models, where each runs and how PaperDaemon starts it, the harness each runs with, and making harnesses.›
  6. 06SearchSearXNG or a search API, and what each social source can and can't do.›
  7. 07Decompose and sendAn image becomes pen strokes and goes to a new page in the inbox notebook.›
  8. 08BenchmarkThe whole pipeline on a mirror of its own, the same cases every run, and what your setup can do.›
  9. 09NetworkPort, listen addresses for WireGuard, the pairing token.›
  10. 10CapabilitiesWhat the host may do on a page, what each capability needs, room for more.›
  11. 11ComponentsEvery component and state on one sheet, for building the real interface.›
